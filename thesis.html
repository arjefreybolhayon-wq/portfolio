The L.I.G.T.A.S. Complex Center (Law Intervention Group Tactic Action Surveillance) is envisioned as an integrated disaster response and management facility, similar in function to Valenzuela City’s ALERT Center, Cagayan de Oro City’s ORO Central, and Pasig City’s RED Center. These facilities are designed to provide local government units (LGUs) with comprehensive support in coordination with the National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council (NDRRMC) and its regional divisions. The L.I.G.T.A.S. Complex Center is proposed as a new-generation disaster response facility capable of responding to emergencies, accommodating evacuees, conducting rescue operations, and providing various training facilities for natural-hazard preparedness and response. Different regions have varying levels of capability and resources for disaster preparedness and emergency response. Binangonan, Rizal, is considered a suitable location for the development of an enhanced disaster response complex capable of supporting day-to-day operations involving security, emergency response, community assistance, evacuation management, medical services, rescue operations, and disaster preparedness training. Binangonan is located along Laguna de Bay and is vulnerable to flooding, particularly during typhoons and periods of intense rainfall. Despite these recurring hazards, some existing developments and essential community facilities are situated in areas exposed to natural risks. Evacuation centers, hospitals, and schools are among the facilities that may be significantly affected during disasters and may lack adequate planning and infrastructure to withstand the impacts of severe natural calamities. The L.I.G.T.A.S. Binangonan Complex Center is envisioned as a centralized command and disaster response facility integrating an evacuation center, medical assistance unit, fire station, police station, rescue operations, and community assistance services, including Red Cross volunteers. The complex aims to provide an organized and coordinated response to disasters while serving as a safe and accessible temporary shelter for affected communities. Through the integration of emergency response, medical assistance, rescue operations, security, evacuation management, and disaster preparedness training, the facility seeks to strengthen Binangonan’s overall capacity to respond effectively to natural and human-induced hazards.
