PROJECT
PORTFOLIO.

This architectural design presents a harmonious fusion of Islamic and Ifugao influences, resulting in a culturally rich and visually striking residence. The structure reflects Islamic architectural principles through its geometric patterns, arches, and emphasis on symmetry, which are subtly expressed in the façade details, balcony openings, and decorative elements. At the same time, the influence of Ifugao vernacular architecture is evident in the elevated massing, wide overhanging roofs, and the use of earthy textures and natural tones that echo traditional highland dwellings. The combination creates a balance between spirituality and environmental responsiveness—where Islamic design brings a sense of order and elegance, while Ifugao elements contribute practicality, climate adaptation, and a deep connection to nature. Together, these two traditions are reinterpreted in a modern context, producing a home that is both culturally meaningful and architecturally innovative.
