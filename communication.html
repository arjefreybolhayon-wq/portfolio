AFP MILITARY BRANCH COMMUNICATION OFFICE BLDG.

The architectural design showcased in the 3D renders features a multi-story, L-shaped modern institutional facility characterized by a striking contemporary aesthetic. The building utilizes a bold color palette dominated by bright green accents, natural wood grain siding, stark white plaster, and dark charcoal grey structural elements. A prominent design feature is the extensive use of large, floor-to-ceiling curtain wall glazing on the main facade, which allows for substantial natural light infiltration while creating a highly transparent, welcoming entrance area. The structure incorporates geometric massing with sharp, clean lines and dynamic architectural elements, such as projecting green frames that wrap around sections of the facade to create depth, and horizontal louvers that provide texture, visual interest, and potential solar shading. Exterior lighting is thoughtfully integrated, featuring dramatic up-and-down sconce lighting along the ground-floor pillars and accent spotlights that accentuate the building's textures and verticality.
