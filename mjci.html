MJCI BLDG: 2 STOREY COMMERCIAL BLDG.

a contemporary low-rise mixed-use commercial building designed with a strong emphasis on transparency, branding, and pedestrian engagement. Architecturally, the structure is characterized by its linear composition and human-scale massing, consisting of approximately two to three storeys that align closely with the street to form an active urban edge. The façade is dominated by large glazed panels framed in metal, allowing visual permeability between interior and exterior spaces while showcasing retail activities at ground level and office functions above. Horizontal elements such as continuous signage bands, canopies, and fascia lines unify the elevation.
