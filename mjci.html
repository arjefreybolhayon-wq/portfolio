MRS. UY CONDOMINIUM

The interior design of this studio condominium showcases a bold, highly textured Industrial Fusion style that cleverly maximizes limited square footage through a series of distinct, functional zones. The overall aesthetic balances raw urban elements with vibrant, high-contrast contemporary accents.

Materiality and Textures:
The space utilizes a diverse palette of tactile finishes to create depth and visual division without relying on full walls. A rustic, red-brick accent wall grounds the compact kitchen, seamlessly transitioning into a multi-textured entertainment hub featuring an integrated television mounted above a faux stone fireplace.

In the sleeping quarters, the design introduces a stark contrast with a white, wavy 3D-textured accent wall, complemented by industrial wireframe pendant lights and black-and-white New York City iconography.

Spatial Zoning and Color Theory:
The studio's layout is meticulously organized using an open-slat partition system finished in alternating matte black and crimson red vertical stripes. This open screen divides the bedroom from the living areas, offering privacy for the bed while allowing light to filter through, and doubles as functional shelving with integrated white shadow boxes for greenery and decor.

The color story is deeply intentional, utilizing a dramatic foundation of deep charcoals, warm natural wood cabinetry, and exposed brick, punctuated by strategic pops of vibrant crimson red found on the dining chairs, the living room armchair, and the room divider.

This energetic accent color successfully unifies the micro-zones— sleeping, lounging, dining, and cooking—into a cohesive, dynamic, and sophisticated urban living space.
