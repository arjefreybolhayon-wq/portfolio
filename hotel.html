SUMMIT HOTEL QUIRINO: 2 STAR HOTEL W/ COMMERCIAL UNITS.

The architectural concept for the Summit Hotel Quirino introduces a vibrant, mixed-use approach to a budget-friendly hospitality development, maximizing both high-street commercial value and contemporary roadside appeal. Designed with a clean, modern geometric profile, the building's asymmetric roofline and sharp, angular charcoal-gray accents contrast beautifully against crisp white concrete volumes. The upper hotel level is defined by a rhythmic series of private balconies, utilizing warm wood-grain panels and sleek glass-and-iron balustrades to create a cozy, premium aesthetic that balances the building's sharp industrial lines. A striking mountain-shaped neon emblem sits proudly at the highest point of the facade, anchoring the hotel's local identity and acting as a visible beacon for passing travelers. The ground level is highly optimized for commercial activity, featuring an open, street-facing arcade layout designed to attract maximum foot traffic.
