RENOVATION OF AFP GYMNASIUM BLDG.

The gymnasium design follows a modern sports and community-centered architectural concept, combining functionality, energy, and strong visual identity. The exterior uses a contemporary composition of clean geometric forms, green structural elements, large glazed surfaces, and dramatic lighting to create an active and welcoming character. The green façade treatment and dynamic patterns suggest movement and athleticism, while the warm wood finishes provide balance and a more inviting atmosphere. Inside, the design emphasizes flexibility and multi-sport functionality, providing spaces for basketball, volleyball, badminton, table tennis, billiards, swimming, and other recreational activities. The main sports hall features a spacious open floor, spectator seating, and an exposed structural ceiling enhanced with colorful linear elements that reflect the energy of sports. Consistent use of blue, green, red, and wood tones creates a cohesive visual identity throughout the facility, while large openings, high ceilings, and abundant artificial lighting help maintain a bright and comfortable environment. Overall, the concept presents the gymnasium as a dynamic, inclusive, and multifunctional sports hub designed to encourage physical activity, competition, recreation, and community interaction.
