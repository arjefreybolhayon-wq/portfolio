MRS. CAYONG CONDOMINIUM

his small condo unit features an efficient, cohesive studio layout that maximizes a "Nordic, Scandi, Industrial, Minimalist" aesthetic. The space flows linearly from a light gray loveseat into the sleeping quarters, utilizing a dark charcoal feature wall to add depth. Warm wood laminates on the cabinetry, accent panels, and floating shelves soften the bold walls, while integrated cove lighting ensures the narrow unit feels bright and welcoming. Opposite the living area, the space prioritizes high functionality without cluttering the floor plan. A compact two-seater dining table sits near the entryway, while a towering wardrobe and full-sized refrigerator are kept flush against the wall to clear the walkway. The media wall is highly optimized, featuring a wall-mounted TV, a slim console, and graphic typography prints that lend a youthful, urban personality to the home. The kitchen zone is a masterclass in compact efficiency. It boasts a striking dual-tone cabinet configuration, combining warm wooden upper cabinets with deep, muted green-gray base units. A sleek black countertop houses a single-basin stainless steel sink and an induction cooktop, while a stainless-steel wall rail keeps cooking utensils and spices neatly organized and off the counter surfaces. The sleeping area is positioned at the far end of the unit next to a large grid window that floods the room with daylight. A neutral-toned bed is styled with comfortable gray and beige pillows, maintaining the unit's restrained color palette. Above the bed, the dark accent wall supports dual-tiered floating shelves for books and decor, while the bright green window frames provide an unexpected, vibrant pop of color against the neutral curtains.
